# Player Warp Rules

---

:::note
> **Effective Date:** July 30th 2026  
> These rules will go into effect on **July 30th 2026**. Pwarps not following the rules below will be removed.  
> **Please note:** Pwarps removed during this period will **not** result in a warning.
:::

---

## 1. Pwarp Sub‑Categories

### 1.1 Available Sub‑Categories
There are **5** sub-categories on the `/pwarp` menu:

- **Generic**
- **Shops**
- **Grinders**
- **Towns**
- **Events**

### 1.2 Category Requirement
Every pwarp must be assigned to one of the sub categories listed above.  
Uncategorised pwarps will be removed during weekly review.

### 1.3 Player Responsibility
Players are responsible for ensuring their pwarp remains correctly categorised at all times.

---

## 2. Generic Pwarp Rules

### 2.1 Weekly Review and Removal
Staff conduct a weekly review and purge of all pwarps.  
Any pwarp that is not assigned to a valid category or is not following the rules of its category will be removed.  
Players who have their pwarps removed will **not** be reimbursed the cost of pwarp creation.

### 2.2 Safety Requirements
- No hostile mobs allowed.  
- Area must be fully claimed.  
- Players must not die upon warping unless clear warning signs are posted.

### 2.3 Creation Fee
Each Pwarp cost increases per pwarp you have.  
The exact formula is:

`$7,000 + (3,000 * ((n) + 1 ^ ((n) + 1)))`

Where **n** is the number of pwarps you currently have.

Prices for the first 5 pwarps are:

1: $10,000  
2: $19,000  
3: $34,000  
4: $55,000  
5: $82,000  

### 2.4 Category Assignment Command
You must assign one of the five categories when setting a Pwarp; uncategorized Pwarps will be removed.

Set a category using:  
`/pw category set (Name) (Generic/Shops/Grinders/Towns/Events)`

Example:  
`/pw category set ExampleShop Shops`

Manage your pwarp using:  
`/pw manage (Name)`

## 3. Shop Pwarp Rules

### 3.1 Minimum Shop Requirement
A Shop Pwarp must contain at least **10** distinct shops.

### 3.2 Stock Requirement
If more than **50%** of shops are out of stock, a **7‑day removal timer** begins.

### 3.3 Donation Box Rule
Donation boxes do **not** count toward the ten shop minimum.

### 3.4 Location Restrictions
Shop pwarps cannot warp players into the same building, room, stall, floor, or shared interior space.  
If two or more shop pwarps lead to the same location, they will be removed.

### 3.5 Shopping Mall Exception
Player‑made shopping malls may use multiple shop pwarps **only when each warp leads to its own clearly separated shop unit**.

If several pwarps point to the same overall build, each warp must:
- follow all shop rules  
- lead to a distinct space separate from the general shared area  

A shop inside a larger themed build is allowed only if it is its own clearly defined shop space, separate from the main shared environment.

---

## 4. Grinder Pwarp Rules

### 4.1 Safe Warp Location
Warp must deposit players in a safe zone.  
Players must then move into the grinder area.  
Immediate deaths are not allowed.

### 4.2 Spawner Requirement
A grinder pwarp must provide access to at least **10** spawners.  
These may be spread across multiple grinder setups.  
If the grinder does not use spawners, it must provide comparable rates.

---

## 5. Town Pwarp Rules

### 5.1 Town Hall Requirement
A town pwarp must have a town hall or welcome area.  
Town rules must be displayed prominently.

### 5.2 Claim Structure
The mayor claims the entire town in one large claim.  
Sub‑claims are used for individual resident builds.

### 5.3 Resident List
All residents must be listed in the welcome area.

### 5.4 Minimum Population
A town must have at least **three** residents plus the **mayor**.

---

## 6. Events Pwarp Rules

### 6.1 Safe Warp Location
Events Pwarps must warp players into a safe arrival area.  
The event or minigame must be clearly defined, fully claimed, and safe to enter.

### 6.2 Function
The event must function properly and be accessible to the public.
